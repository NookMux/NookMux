---
title: Dynamic Ratios
---

The `/dynamic-ratio` route is used by administrators to configure rules that override billing ratios based on conditions.

Dynamic ratios are useful for temporary promotions, specific model/group combinations, and special user-group policies. They work with base model prices, model ratios, and group ratios to determine final charges.

After configuring, verify charges with low-traffic requests and usage logs to avoid billing issues.
