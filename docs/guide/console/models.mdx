---
title: Model Management
---

The `/models/metadata` route is used to maintain model metadata, vendor information, and deployment configuration.

Common capabilities:

- Manage model names, vendors, categories, icons, descriptions, and visibility.
- Configure model deployments, access rules, and pre-filled groups.
- Sync or import upstream model metadata.
- Integrate with public pricing pages and model selectors.

Pricing and ratios are not maintained here. Configure them in **System Settings → Billing & Payments → Model Pricing / Group Pricing**.
