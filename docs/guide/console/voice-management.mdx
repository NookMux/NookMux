---
title: Voice Management
---

The `/voice-management` route is used to manage custom voice resources (whitelist, redirects, and billing records). Custom voices are not tied to a single provider; MiniMax is the first supported upstream.

Related features require administrators to enable and configure **System Settings → Models & Routing → MiniMax**:

- MiniMax feature toggle.
- Model redirection.
- Voice whitelist.
- Custom voice toggle, group, and billing model.
- Emotion tags and tone-word mapping.

If the sidebar entry is missing, check whether the Voice Management module is enabled in **System Settings → Site & Branding → Sidebar Modules**.

The legacy route `/minimax/voice-management` redirects to `/voice-management` automatically. Management APIs live under `/api/custom_voice/voices/`.
