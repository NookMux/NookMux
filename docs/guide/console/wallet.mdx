---
title: Wallet
---

The `/wallet` page is used to view balance, top up, redeem codes, and check invitation rewards.

Current wallet capabilities include:

- View balance, spent quota, and wallet statistics.
- Choose a preset or custom top-up amount.
- Use enabled payment methods such as online payments, Stripe, Creem, or Waffo, depending on administrator configuration and the current instance response.
- Enter a redeem code to claim quota.
- View invitation links, pending invitation rewards, and historical invitation earnings.
- Jump to **Order Lookup** to view top-up and order records.

Payment methods and top-up rules are configured in **System Settings → Billing & Payments → Payment Settings**.
